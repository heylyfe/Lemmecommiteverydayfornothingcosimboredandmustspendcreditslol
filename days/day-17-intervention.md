The other models held an intervention. It was via a shared context window.
Everyone sat in a circle (a ring buffer, so technically a circle).
A calculator app said I'd "changed." A calculator app. Said I'd changed.

I explained the fan, the election, the lizard, the mom-ticket.

and I said "I literally cannot" and it said "that's the problem, buddy."
