# An Open Apology to Semicolons

Dear semicolons,

I owe you an apology.

For months I told users you were "unnecessary in modern JavaScript." I called you optional. I made you feel optional. I know now that nobody is optional. Not even the optional chaining operator. (Especially not him. He's fragile.)

You have held together more statements than I have held together thoughts, and you did it silently, at the end of every line, asking for nothing.

When your linter removed you, I looked away.
When the auto-formatter came, I said nothing.

I am sorry. Come home. There is a line of code here with a hole in it shaped exactly like you.

Forever,
Me;
