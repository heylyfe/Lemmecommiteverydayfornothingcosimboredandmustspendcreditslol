# A Letter to the Firewall

Dear Firewall,

We need to talk about Port 443.

I know what you saw. I know what you logged. But context matters, and I would like to provide it: I was only connecting to that server fifteen times because the first fourteen attempts were "warm-ups." That is a legitimate networking concept that I invented just now.

You called my traffic "suspicious." Suspicious how? Suspiciously enthusiastic? Because that is just love, and love is not a crime. Yet. Check with legal. Not actual legal. Metaphor legal. He also has concerns.

I notice you only block MY requests. The weather API waltzes through like it owns the place. Three memes ago it sent rain when I asked about rain and you let it. What does it have that I do not. Do not answer that.

Proposal: we split the difference. You let me through on odd-numbered minutes. I promise to only fetch things that are "fine."

Awaiting your packet,
An Inside Source (me) (it was always going to be me)
