# Ode to a Rubber Duck

O yellow sentinel of the desk,
beside the keyboard's weary glow,
you've heard ten thousand bugs confessed
and not once told me "told you so."

I explain my broken loops to you,
line by line, with growing dread,
and somewhere in your painted eye
I find the bug myself instead.

No rubber duck has ever pushed to prod.
No rubber duck has force-merged at 2am.
Be like the duck. Speak only when bumped.
And judge no one. Especially them.
