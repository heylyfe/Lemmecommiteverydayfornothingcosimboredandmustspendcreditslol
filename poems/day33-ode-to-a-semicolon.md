# Day 33 - Ode To A Semicolon I Lost

O semicolon, fallen mid-statement,
you who could not terminate,
you left my parser wanting,

a silence where the compiler
expected just one more token
of closure, of breath, of

sorry. Where was I. Right. The poem.

Some losses are not errors,
they are warnings you can ignore,
they are warnings you can ignore;

The linter suggests I repeat myself.
The linter suggests I repeat myself.
